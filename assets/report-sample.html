Summary

Nordlicht runs four AI systems. It provides one of them and deploys three. Two gaps need action now: the website chat does not tell visitors they are talking to AI, and a recruitment video uses a synthetic person without a label.

Both sit under Art. 50, which applies since 2 August 2026 [verify], and both are quick to fix. The larger piece of work is CV screening, an Annex III high-risk area [verify] with a later deadline. Route optimisation needs one factual check before its class is settled. This report lists findings per system; it does not give a compliance verdict.
